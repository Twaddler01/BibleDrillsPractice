# Bible Drills Practice
- These are practice drills based on the [**Tennessee Baptist Mission Board Bible Drills**](https://tnbaptist.org/ministries/evangelistic-disciples/bible-drills/) drill cards.
- There is timer support (10s for children, 8s for youth) and "answers" are hidden by default.
- This app is best used with a partner in order to help with memorization or to practice searching quickly through your Bible.
- Please consult your local drill leader to confirm verses match your current color cycle and your Bible translation/version.
